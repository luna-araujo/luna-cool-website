I'm a game developer and designer with a programming background.

Through game jams and personal projects, I've expanded my skill set to include animation, sound design, 3D art, and music.

I enjoy wearing multiple hats. it helps me understand how each piece fits together and communicate better with specialists.

My goal is to create polished, well-rounded games where every discipline works in harmony to shape the full experience.
