High Bounty is a Wild West-themed slot by Caleta Gaming, released August 2026. Symbols pay anywhere on the grid and tumble after every win, keeping the showdown alive across combinations, while scatters crack the vault open into Free Spins, where Multiplier symbols raise the value of each round's wins. Medium volatility, 94.21% RTP, and a maximum win of 11,097x. Features include Pay Anywhere, Scatter, Free Spins, Multiplier, and Ante Bet. I contributed to the design and development at Caleta.
