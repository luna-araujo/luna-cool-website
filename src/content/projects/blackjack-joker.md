Blackjack Joker is a table game by Caleta Gaming, released March 2026. Played with eight decks, it offers a 3:2 blackjack payout, 2:1 insurance, and flexible betting limits with multi-hand gameplay. Features include Double Down, Insurance, and Splits. I contributed to the design and development at Caleta.
