Cluster Cup is a high-volatility soccer-themed slot by Caleta Gaming, released April 2026, featuring a dynamic 7x7 cluster grid. With a progressive multiplier, free spins, and chained cluster wins that unlock super features after 3+ tumblings, it offers a max win of 8,600x. Features include Tumbling, Big Symbol, Multiplier, Wild, Free Spins, Cluster, and Scatter. I contributed to the design and development at Caleta.
